Licences and credits
====================

RA4 Light Lab (RA4追光暗房) is free software, built on SpektraLab. This folder
carries the full text of every licence the app is distributed under, because a
binary that ships without them is not licensed to be shipped.

  GPL-3.0.txt

      to the corresponding source code of this app:
      https://github.com/lyolomeister/SpektraLab
      The engine comes from SpektraLab by Hanze Qiu:
      https://github.com/JamesQiu2005/SpektraLab

  Profiles-and-LUTs-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt
      The colour film and paper profiles, and the print-preview LUTs derived
      from them, by Andrea Volpato, CC BY-SA 4.0.
      https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm
      Redistribution and derivatives must credit the author, link the
      project, and preserve that licence.

  Profiles-and-LUTs-CHANGELOG.txt
      What SpektraLab did and did not change about them. The profiles are
      unmodified; the LUTs are derivatives, and this says how they were made.
      RA4 uses the same files, unchanged.

  metal-cpp-Apache-2.0.txt
      metal-cpp, Apple's C++ bindings for Metal, Apache-2.0, compiled into
      the app.

The black-and-white film and paper profiles are SpektraLab's own, by Hanze
Qiu; the engine's PRODUCT_PROFILES_NOTICE.md says what each is built from.

nor by any maker of the films and papers whose names it shows.
